## For businesses where sales relies on memory

When conversations are spread across messages, notes and inboxes, it becomes harder to know who needs a response and what should happen next. A useful sales system makes that work visible.

## What we can work through

- How an enquiry moves from first contact to a decision.
- The information needed to understand an opportunity.
- Clear next actions and responsibility for follow-ups.
- A practical rhythm for reviewing open conversations.

## Start with the process you actually use

We discuss the current flow, identify where it becomes unclear and agree a manageable scope for improvement. The approach should fit the way your business works and the tools you already use.

A system is useful when people can keep using it. The focus is on clear steps and consistent follow-through, with the specific deliverables agreed before work begins.
