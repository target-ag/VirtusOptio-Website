## Better conversations. Stronger relationships.

I’m Mark Deniel, the person behind Virtus Optio. I help businesses turn more sales conversations into customers through ethical selling and practical sales systems.

My focus is on the work between an initial conversation and a lasting client relationship: understanding what someone needs, making the next step clear, and following through.

## How I approach sales

Selling starts with listening. A useful conversation gives both sides enough information to decide whether working together makes sense. That means being clear about what you can deliver and being comfortable when an offer is not the right fit.

A practical sales system supports that approach. It gives business owners a way to keep track of conversations and follow-ups without having to remember every detail themselves.

## What we can work on

- [Ethical selling](services/ethical-selling.md): understanding needs and communicating value clearly.
- [Sales systems](services/sales-systems.md): making follow-ups and next steps more consistent.
- [Client relationships](services/client-relationships.md): keeping the conversation going after a sale.
