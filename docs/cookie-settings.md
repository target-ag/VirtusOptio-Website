There are currently **no optional advertising or analytics cookies** on this website. You do not need to accept tracking to browse it.

When you use the theme switch, this browser saves your choice of light or dark mode. The control below removes only that saved theme preference and returns this page to light mode. It does not clear other sites' data or settings in WhatsApp, LinkedIn or your email application.

Clearing a preference does not prevent you from choosing a theme again. Using the theme switch afterwards saves your new choice.

No cookie banner interrupts your visit. The site removes the obsolete acknowledgement saved by earlier versions when browser storage is available.

Read the [Cookie Policy](cookies.md) and [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) for details about functional storage, hosting and security.
