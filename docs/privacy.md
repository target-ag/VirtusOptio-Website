## Information we use and why

### Visiting this website

This is an informational website with a blog. It has no visitor accounts, checkout, contact form, embedded chat widget, advertising pixels or visitor analytics scripts. Fonts, images, styles and scripts are served from the website itself.

Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website. Providing and protecting the site involves technical request information, which can include IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information, timestamps and security signals. Cloudflare also enables network-error reporting: supported browsers can send reports when a request fails. This is used for delivery, reliability and security, not for advertising on this site.

The basis for this technical processing is the legitimate interest in operating a reliable and secure website. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and [network-error reporting explanation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/network-error-logging/) for the provider's practices.

### Your display preference

If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser stores that choice locally. The site uses it to remember the appearance you requested. It is not a visitor identifier and is not sent to us by the theme feature. See the [Cookie Policy](cookies.md) and [Cookie Settings](cookie-settings.md).

### Contacting us and working together

If you email or message us, we receive the information you choose to share. This can include your name, business contact details, profile information, message content and details of your business challenge. For an engagement, relevant correspondence, agreement and invoicing information may also be needed.

We use this information to respond, discuss an assignment, deliver agreed services and manage the business relationship. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract or performing a contract where you are a party, or our legitimate interest in managing business enquiries and relationships where you represent an organisation. Records required by law are processed to meet those legal obligations.

Please keep initial enquiries relevant to your request and avoid sending customer databases or sensitive personal information. Where an assignment requires us to process data on a client's behalf, a separate [Data Processing Agreement](data-processing-agreement.md) must describe that processing.

## How long information is kept

Retention depends on the purpose and the type of record. Enquiry information is kept for handling the enquiry and any necessary follow-up. Engagement and administrative records may need to remain available for the assignment, legal obligations or legal claims. The criteria are whether the information remains necessary for those purposes and whether a legal retention period applies. Technical records held by providers follow the relevant service's retention arrangements.

The saved theme preference has no automatic expiry. You can remove it through [Cookie Settings](cookie-settings.md) or your browser's site-data controls.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or transfer of your data. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. If a particular activity relies on your consent, you can withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send a request to [virtusoptio@gmail.com](mailto:virtusoptio@gmail.com). We may need proportionate information to verify that the request concerns you; please do not send an identity document with your initial message. GDPR requests are normally answered within one month. If a lawful extension is needed, we will explain it.

You can also complain to the [Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens)](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/en) or your local data protection authority.
