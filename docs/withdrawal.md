## Services for businesses

Virtus Optio provides freelance services exclusively to business clients acting for professional or commercial purposes.

The statutory consumer cooling-off period of 14 days generally **does not apply to these business agreements**. Cancellation, rescheduling and termination are governed by the agreed proposal and applicable [Terms and Conditions](terms.md).
