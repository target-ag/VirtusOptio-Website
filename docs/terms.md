## Agreeing an engagement

An engagement starts when we agree the proposal or other written engagement terms. Those terms identify the services, deliverables, fees, timing and responsibilities. Visiting this website or sending an enquiry does not by itself create a paid engagement.

These general terms apply when they are provided and agreed as part of the engagement. A specific written agreement takes precedence over these general terms where the two differ. We will make the applicable terms available before the engagement is concluded.

Sales results also depend on factors outside our control; an engagement does not guarantee a particular amount of revenue or number of customers.

The client is responsible for providing accurate information, timely decisions and lawful access to the systems and materials needed for the assignment. Both parties remain responsible for complying with the laws that apply to their own activities.

Changes to the scope, deliverables, timetable or fees should be agreed in writing before the additional or revised work starts.

The proposal or engagement agreement specifies the fees, any applicable VAT, approved expenses, invoicing arrangements and payment deadline. Additional costs require agreement. If something on an invoice appears incorrect, please contact us promptly so we can resolve it.

Our [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) explains how we handle website and enquiry information.
