# The Only 3 Ways to Grow a Business (And How to Use Them)

<img src="/assets/blog/three-ways-grow-business-680.webp" srcset="/assets/blog/three-ways-grow-business-480.webp 480w, /assets/blog/three-ways-grow-business-680.webp 680w, /assets/blog/three-ways-grow-business-1360.webp 1360w" sizes="(max-width: 731px) calc(100vw - 51px), 680px" alt="The three ways to grow a business: more customers, greater value per sale, and more frequent purchases." width="1672" height="941" decoding="async" fetchpriority="high">


**TL;DR:** Growing your business doesn't always mean spending more on marketing or chasing more leads. There are three fundamental but highly profitable ways to increase your revenue:

1. **Get more customers** — Turn more prospects into paying customers.

2. **Increase the value of each sale** — Generate more revenue from every transaction.

3. **Get customers to buy more often** — Build stronger relationships that lead to repeat business.

**The real opportunity?** Small improvements in all three areas can compound into significant revenue growth.

Read on to discover how to put these three growth levers to work in your business.

<!-- more -->

---

Most business owners are faced with a paradox:
**The more you want to grow, the more your business seems to need you.**

More leads mean more conversations. More opportunities mean more follow-ups. And more sales often mean more of your time.

Eventually, you face a question that every ambitious business owner needs to answer:

**How do you increase sales without having to personally make every deal happen?**

The obvious answer might be to hire more salespeople, generate more leads, or spend more on marketing.

But what if you're overlooking opportunities that already exist inside your business?

What if the next stage of growth doesn't begin with finding more prospects, but with getting more value from the opportunities and customers you already have?

This is where a surprisingly simple principle comes in.

## No matter what you sell, there are only three ways to grow your revenue.

_Three Ways to Grow a Business_ framework breaks them down into three levers:

1. **Increase the number of customers** — Convert more of your opportunities into paying customers.

2. **Increase the average transaction value** — Generate more revenue from each sale.

3. **Increase purchase frequency** — Give customers more reasons to buy from you again.


Simple enough.

But here's what makes these three levers particularly interesting.

## You don't need dramatic improvements in any one of them to create meaningful growth.

Improve all three by just 10%, and the combined effect is a **33.1% increase in revenue**.

And that's before considering where your business might be leaving money on the table today.

Of course, this framework isn't designed specifically to remove the owner from the sales process.

But it does something equally valuable: it reveals where growth opportunities exist and where you can build more repeatable sales processes.

Instead of immediately asking, _"Where can we get more leads?"_, you start asking three better questions:

* Where are we losing potential customers?
* Where are we missing opportunities to deliver more value?
* And why aren't more of our existing customers buying again?

**The answers might change where you focus your sales efforts next.**

Let's examine each of the three growth levers, and how you can start putting them to work.

## 1. Increase the Number of Customers

The first way to grow your business is to **turn more prospects into paying customers.**

Most businesses immediately think about generating more leads. But more leads won't necessarily produce more sales if your conversion rate is low.

Before spending more on marketing, look at the opportunities you already have.

Are prospects receiving timely follow-ups? Does your sales team understand their needs? Can they clearly communicate why customers should choose you?

**For example:** If you receive 100 qualified leads and convert 10%, you gain 10 customers. Improve your conversion rate to 15%, and you gain 15 customers—**50% more customers without generating a single additional lead.**

The takeaway: **Don't just focus on attracting more prospects. Get better at converting the ones you already have.**

## 2. Increase the Average Value of Each Sale

The second way to grow is to **increase how much customers spend each time they buy.**

Many businesses focus so heavily on closing the sale that they overlook opportunities to provide additional value.

Think about what your customers need beyond their initial purchase.

Could you offer a premium version? Bundle complementary services? Recommend something that makes their original purchase more effective?

The goal isn't to push customers into spending more. It's to help them solve a bigger part of their problem.

**For example:** If you make 100 sales at an average value of €1,000, you generate €100,000. Increase the average sale to €1,200, and revenue rises to €120,000—**20% more revenue from the same number of transactions.**

The takeaway: **Don't just ask how to close more sales. Ask how each sale can deliver more value.**

## 3. Increase How Often Customers Buy

The third way to grow is to **encourage existing customers to buy from you again.**

Acquiring a customer is only the beginning of the relationship. Yet many businesses invest heavily in winning new customers while neglecting the ones they've already earned.

Stay in touch. Follow up after the purchase. Understand what customers need next, and make relevant offers when the time is right.

Depending on your business, this could mean renewals, repeat orders, maintenance, additional services, or upgrades.

**For example:** If 100 customers spend €1,000 twice a year, you generate €200,000. If they purchase three times instead, revenue grows to €300,000—**50% more revenue without acquiring another customer.**

Of course, repeat purchases must reflect genuine customer needs.

The takeaway: **Don't treat the first sale as the finish line. Treat it as the beginning of a longer customer relationship.**

## The Real Opportunity: Combine All Three

Here's where the framework becomes especially powerful.

Imagine you improve each growth lever by just 10%:

- 10% more customers.

- 10% higher average transaction value.

- 10% more frequent purchases.


Because these improvements multiply rather than simply add together, **your revenue increases by 33.1%.**

That's the power of focusing on all three levers instead of relying entirely on new leads.

And for business owners who still handle too much of the selling themselves, there's another benefit.

Each lever gives you an opportunity to build a repeatable process that your team can execute, measure, and improve.

**Because sustainable growth isn't about working harder to close more deals. It's about building a business that can generate more sales without relying on you every step of the way.**

So, where is the biggest untapped growth opportunity in your business?

1. Winning more customers

2. Increasing the value of each sale

3. Getting existing customers to buy more often
