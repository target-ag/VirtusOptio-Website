## For businesses that want to stay close to customers

Winning a customer is the start of a relationship. Thoughtful follow-up helps you understand whether expectations are being met and where further support would be useful.

## What we can work through

- When and how to check in after a sale.
- Questions that reveal changing needs and expectations.
- Ways to make the next conversation relevant to the customer.
- A consistent approach to keeping track of commitments.

## Build a useful follow-up rhythm

We begin with how you stay in touch today and where relationships lose momentum. We can then agree which touchpoints and habits to improve, keeping the customer’s needs at the centre of the conversation.

Repeat business should follow from a useful offer and a good fit. The goal is to understand the next need, rather than push another purchase.
